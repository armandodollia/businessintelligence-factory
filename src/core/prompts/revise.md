# Revision round

An independent reviewer examined your deliverable and raised the issues below. Handle every issue:

- For each issue call `respond_to_review` once with its `issue_id`:
  - `fix`: you changed the deliverable; say what changed in `reason`.
  - `pushback`: you are not changing it; give a concrete reason.
- Push back (with a reason) on issues classed `nit` or `scope_expansion` instead of doing extra work.
  Fix `blocking` and `substantive` issues unless they are factually wrong; then push back with evidence.
- Save new evidence and findings as needed (same rules as before).
- Finish with `submit_result` carrying the COMPLETE revised deliverable (not a diff).
